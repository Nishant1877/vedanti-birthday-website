# 💖 A Journey to November 14th • For Vedanti

A 14-chapter interactive romantic birthday web experience crafted exclusively for Vedanti.

## ✨ Features Across 14 Chapters
- **Chapter 1: The Mysterious Invitation** - Secret keyhole door with peep portal & golden key unlock
- **Chapter 2: The Cosmic Countdown** - Real-time countdown to midnight November 14th & rapid love meter
- **Chapter 3: Day One (The First Spark)** - Developing Polaroid photo memory with interactive stickers
- **Chapter 4: Chapters in Time** - Dual-tone milestone journey with secret love notes
- **Chapter 5: Hall of Giggle & Grace** - 3D tap-to-flip candid cards & interactive meme soundboard
- **Chapter 6: Our Coordinates** - Travel maps & love metric calculator
- **Chapter 7: The Birthday Fortune Wheel** - Carnival fortune wheel with claimable boyfriend vouchers
- **Chapter 8: Lovers' Trivia Challenge** - 5-question couple quiz with lifeline call
- **Chapter 9: The Jar of 100 Little Reasons** - Glass jar with 100 handwritten romantic reasons & random reason cannon
- **Chapter 10: Scratch to Reveal (Love Lottery)** - 3-zone metallic gold scratch foil physics game
- **Chapter 11: Written in the Stars** - Scorpio constellation connector & shooting star wish cannon
- **Chapter 12: Our Future Bucket List** - Interactive wishlist with persistent localStorage
- **Chapter 13: Virtual Cake & Candles** - Interactive birthday cake with real-time mic blow / tap candle extinguish
- **Chapter 14: The Royal Vault & Letter** - Velvet 3D unboxing, wax-sealed love letter & living video reels

## 🚀 100% Pure Static Frontend
- Zero backend dependencies.
- Mobile-first responsive design (iPhone & Android safe-area aware).
- Ready for instant deployment on **Vercel**, **Netlify**, or **GitHub Pages**.
